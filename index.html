Privacy Policy for Eyeji - Screen Distance Utility Last Updated: [26/12/25] Eyeji ("we", "our", or "us") is a utility application designed to help users maintain a specific viewing distance from their device screen. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your data. 1. Data Collection and Usage We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. The application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not have servers, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising services that collect user data. 2. Camera Permission & Usage To function as a distance measuring tool, Eyeji requires access to your device's Camera. How it is used: The app analyzes video frames in real-time to estimate the distance between your face and the screen. Data Handling: All processing happens instantly on your device's processor. No images, videos, or facial data are ever recorded, saved, or transmitted to us or any third party. Facial Recognition: We detect the presence of a face to calculate distance. We do not use facial recognition to identify who you are. 3. Background Service Eyeji uses a Foreground Service to continue measuring distance when you are using other apps or on the home screen. Notification: A persistent notification is displayed whenever this service is active, ensuring you are always aware that the measurement tool is running. Control: You can stop this service at any time using the main toggle switch in the app. 4. Children’s Privacy Our application is a general utility tool. It does not target children under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect any personal information from children. 5.
